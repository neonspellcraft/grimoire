Grimoire for Windows
====================

Run grimoire.exe. The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't
code-signed yet: click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Add music by dragging audio files or folders onto the window, or click the + in the controls row.
MP3, WAV, AIFF, FLAC and .m3u playlists are supported.

Main window
  Click the prompt to search the playlist; Enter plays the match, Esc clears it.
  Click or drag the seek line and the volume; scroll the list; drag rows to reorder; double-click to play.
  The gear button opens settings: keyboard shortcuts, the visualizer scene, band meters and reseed.
  Ctrl+Q quits. The keyboard's media keys and the Windows media overlay work too.

Keyboard shortcuts (off by default; turn them on in settings)
  Space play/pause, Left/Right seek 5 s (Shift: 30 s), Up/Down select, Enter play, Delete removes,
  type anywhere to search, Ctrl+Left/Right previous/next, Ctrl+O open files, Ctrl+V visualizer window.
  Visualizer window: 1-4 or Tab switch scenes, R reseed, M band meters, F full screen.

Visualizer window (the diamond button)
  Double-click for full screen; Esc leaves it.

Settings and the playlist are saved in %APPDATA%\Grimoire.
